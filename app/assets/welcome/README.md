## Edit and playback

Scene one uses source seconds 1–5 (1920×1080, 24 fps), cropped to 840×1080 at x=720, y=0. Scene two uses source seconds 12–16 (1080×1920, 25 fps), cropped to 1080×1388 at x=0, y=420. Both are resized to 672×864 with square pixels, concatenated with one clean cut, and encoded H.264/yuv420p at 24 fps in a fast-start MP4. Audio is removed, not merely muted. The JPEG poster is the first frame of the montage. Combined assets are under 1 MB. Both are bundled; playback makes no external media requests and adds no analytics.

Playback is decorative and only mounted while the welcome route is focused, the app is active, Reduce Motion is off, and the person has not paused it. Until the accessibility preference resolves, the poster is used. Error or a first-frame timeout of 2.5 seconds permanently selects the poster for that screen visit. Leaving the screen or backgrounding unmounts and releases the player; returning while still on welcome creates a new player. The CTA is always usable, including before video loads.
