Lean-Transfer Fall 2026 Demo Day

Thursday, November 19, 2026 · 4:00 PM – 6:00 PM

Where deep tech meets market impact. Lean-Transfer is a Berkeley Haas course in which teams of MBA, engineering and science graduate students use Lean Launchpad methods to test the commercial potential of inventions from UC Berkeley, UCSF, Berkeley Lab, Los Alamos National Laboratory and NASA.

At Demo Day the cross-disciplinary teams give their final pitches on the startup concepts they have built around these technologies. Expect live judging with audience voting, a look at breakthrough research from Berkeley labs, and networking with founders and inventors afterward. The winning team receives $10,000 in prize funding from Javelin Ventures.

Registration required. Hosted by Berkeley StEP.

Contact: Lynsey Inthasone, linthasone@berkeley.edu
