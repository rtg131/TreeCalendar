StEP Showcase Day: Berkeley’s Next Wave of Founders Takes the Stage!

DAR
Dec
02

Spieker Forum, Chou Hall, UC Berkeley Haas School of Business |
December 2 @ 5:30 pm - 9:00 pm

Big ideas. Bold founders. 

The next generation of Berkeley startups. Join us for Berkeley StEP Showcase Day, where the Fall 2026 cohort takes the stage to pitch their ventures to a panel of venture capital judges and compete for $6,000 in prizes! 

From AI and HealthTech to CleanTech, consumer products, and social impact, these student founders have spent 10 weeks transforming ambitious ideas into promising startups. 

Why attend? Get a front-row seat to Berkeley’s emerging startups, hear live pitches, and connect with investors, entrepreneurs, and the Berkeley innovation community. 

With $22 million raised by StEP alumni and startups advancing to Y Combinator, SkyDeck, and UC Launch, you never know which future industry leader you’ll meet! 

Come for the pitches. Stay for the connections. Leave inspired. 

👉 Register for Showcase Day Presented by the Berkeley Haas

Entrepreneurship Program, with support from O’Melveny and Swift Ventures
