🚀 Are you interested in biotech entrepreneurship?  Do you have a big idea but don’t know where to start?  Looking for mentorship, teammates, or resources to bring your ideas to life? 

Join us for an in-person info session about Nucleate’s Activator program—a student-led, nonprofit initiative that helps scientists and aspiring entrepreneurs translate cutting-edge research into biotech ventures. Several companies from the Bay Area Activator cohorts include Junevity and Vitra Labs, which have gone on to raise millions in seed funding. 

At this session, you’ll discover how the Activator program connects early academic innovators with mentors and investors and learn how you can apply to take part in this year’s program.  

👉 RSVP here for the Info Session: https://luma.com/8k7mtz52 

Link to Activator Application: https://gateway.nucleate.org/activator-applications  

If you’re curious about startups, considering commercializing your research, or just want to explore the biotech ecosystem, you won’t want to miss it. We can’t wait to see you there!
