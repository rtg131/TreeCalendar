About Event

​Land your first 100 customers with GTM leaders from Workday, Clay, Blitzscaling Ventures, Virio, Resemble AI, Teleport and Imprezia (YC S25)

​Join the GTM Breakthrough Summit on Nov 17 in San Francisco if you’re a pre-seed to Series A B2B founder with a product live and a few design partners or pilots, and you’re stuck turning them into repeatable revenue.

​Who this is for

  • ​Pre-seed to Series A B2B and AI founders with a product in market

  • ​Founder-led sales, somewhere between your first paying customer and your first 100

  • ​Investors and GTM operators who back and advise founders at this stage

​Three things make this evening different

  1. ​Every session tackles one blocker early B2B founders hit right now: distribution when code is cheap, founder-led content, GTM engineering, and turning design partners into paying customers. You leave with tactics you can use the next morning.

  2. ​Every speaker has built, scaled, funded or advised go-to-market at B2B companies, from early-stage startups to Workday.

  3. ​Real opportunities in the room: 5 founders will each be selected for $25K in development support from Loop8. There are also 10 rooftop demo tables and 2 minute pitches with live investor Q&A.

​SPEAKER BIOS

​🎤 Jeremiah Owyang — General Partner, Blitzscaling Ventures

​Jeremiah Owyang is a Silicon Valley venture capitalist, technology thought leader, and General Partner at Blitzscaling Ventures, investing in early-stage AI startups with the potential to become market leaders.

​Working alongside Chris Yeh, co-author of the bestselling book Blitzscaling, Jeremiah brings deep insights into distribution, viral growth, and network effects.

​He is also the founder of Llama Lounge, a popular AI startup event series connecting exceptional founders and investors.

​A sought-after speaker at TED AI, Web Summit, and TechCrunch Disrupt, Jeremiah will share what it takes to build competitive advantages and scale in an increasingly competitive AI landscape.

​Eric Lay — CEO, Virio (executive ghostwriting building inbound for B2B founders)

​𝗘𝗿𝗶𝗰 𝗟𝗮𝘆, pro gamer turned co-founder & CEO of Virio, one of the sharpest voices on founder-led content that actually drives pipeline

​GTM Engineering - Jordan Schuetz From the person who coined the term

​Jordan Schuetz works closely with the operators and builders turning go-to-market into an engineering discipline, from centralizing GTM data to running AI research agents against it to building outbound systems that scale. Before Clay, he led product marketing at TRM Labs and ran developer programs at Twilio, HashiCorp, and MuleSoft, where technical depth and go-to-market meet.

​Clay is the go-to-market platform for revenue teams and the GTM engineers building their pipelines. With 200+ data providers in one place, AI research agents, and tools to turn raw data into personalized outreach at scale, Clay helps high-growth teams build pipeline fast, backed by a community that turned go-to-market into an engineering discipline.

​Indy Bains, Fortune 500 GTM Executive
Global Vice President of Partner, Services & Industry Solution Marketing at Workday

​Indy leads go-to-market across product marketing, industry solutions, partner and ecosystem marketing, and services at Workday, covering ERP, HCM and emerging AI. Before Workday he spent many years at Oracle building cloud ERP and taking it to market. Over his career he has worked in finance, solution engineering, field leadership, product marketing and global GTM. He believes the best growth strategies tie product, sales, marketing, partners and customer outcomes together instead of optimizing each one on its own. He speaks and writes regularly on enterprise AI, ERP and how ecosystems drive growth.

​Claudia Ring, VP of Marketing at Resemble AI

​Claudia specializes in positioning technical products for technical buyers and growing their adoption. She has led marketing and growth at Deepgram, HockeyStack, ActiveState and Aerospike. At Deepgram she layered a product-led motion on top of an existing sales motion and grew pipeline 30% quarter over quarter. She also advises FullyRamped.

​Gurvansh Singh, Founding Growth at Imprezia (YC S25)

​Gurvansh leads growth at Imprezia (YC S25), the world’s first AI native ad network, where he grew users across its partner network from 0 to 15M+ and 10x’d revenue month over month. Before Imprezia, he generated $9.8M+ in qualified pipeline in sales development and business development at Modern Treasury, and was its fastest ramped rep.

​Keith Lam

​Keith Lam mentors at Funding Breakthrough Lab, Berkeley SkyDeck and Bronco Ventures. He’s also a strategic Advisor for Rebel GTM.

​About Our Hosts

​Funding Breakthrough Lab

​Funding Breakthrough Lab (FBL) is a highly selective, no-equity pre-accelerator and 501(c)(3) nonprofit bringing together exceptional founders, accomplished investors, and experienced Silicon Valley operators.

​With a community of 25,000+, FBL creates access to high-caliber expertise, meaningful connections, and opportunities through thoughtfully curated events and founder programs.

​Our Spring 2027 pre-accelerator will select just 40–50 promising founders to receive mentorship, expert guidance, and access to a powerful network to help them build enduring companies.

​We believe exceptional founders can come from anywhere. Talent is everywhere; access to opportunity is not.
