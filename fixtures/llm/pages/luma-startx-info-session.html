About Event

​StartX programs accelerate Stanford entrepreneurs through a uniquely founder-led model that combines deep mentorship, peer-to-peer accountability, and a trusted community that moves fast and shares honestly.

​During this session, you'll hear about:

  • ​StartX and their programs

  • ​The application process and key timelines

  • ​What StartX looks for in successful applicants

​You’ll also have the opportunity to ask questions and connect directly with the team.
