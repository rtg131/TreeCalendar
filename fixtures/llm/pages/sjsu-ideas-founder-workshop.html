San Jose Consulting Group and the IDEAS Entrepreneurship club are partnering to host an interactive workshop! This workshop will bring together student founders and student consultants to give them the opportunity to improve an active startup idea.
The event will include presentations from student founders, breakout sessions, insights from mentors and networking.
Join us for an evening of learning and excitement!
