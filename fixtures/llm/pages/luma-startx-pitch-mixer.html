About Event

​As we wrap up an incredible 2026, KSC Silicon Valley and StartX invite you to a special evening of pitching, connecting, and celebrating together.

​Since our partnership began right after KSC’s grand opening, we’ve hosted some amazing programs alongside StartX. This final open event of the year is all about celebrating the founders, partners, and innovators who have been part of that journey. This final open event of the year is all about celebrating the founders, partners, and innovators who have been part of that journey.

​Why you should pitch (and bring your A-game): While this is a casual and celebratory gathering, we are opening the doors to the wider StartX - KSC community. That means you never know who might be in the room. 🤫 Whether they are top-tier founders, mentors, or investors, this is your chance to showcase your passion and leave a lasting impression.

​Come share your story, catch up with great people, and celebrate the year's end with the KSC and StartX family!
