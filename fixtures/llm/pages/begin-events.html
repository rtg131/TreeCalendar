NOV192026

4:00 PM – 6:00 PM · Chou Hall, Room N170, Berkeley Haas

Lean-Transfer Fall 2026 Demo Day

Where deep tech meets market impact. Lean-Transfer is a Berkeley Haas course in which teams of MBA, engineering and science graduate students use Lean Launchpad methods to test the commercial potential of inventions from UC Berkeley, UCSF, Berkeley Lab, Los Alamos National Laboratory and NASA. At Demo Day the cross-disciplinary teams give their final pitches on the startup concepts they have built around these technologies. Expect live judging with audience voting, a look at breakthrough research from Berkeley labs, and networking with founders and inventors afterward. The winning team receives $10,000 in prize funding from Javelin Ventures. Registration required. Hosted by Berkeley StEP. Contact: Lynsey Inthasone, linthasone@berkeley.edu

DEC22026

6:00 PM – 9:00 PM · Spieker Forum, Chou Hall (6th floor), Berkeley Haas

Berkeley StEP Showcase Day: Fall 2026 Cohort

Berkeley StEP Showcase Day is the finale of StEP's 10-week incubator. This fall's student founder teams take the stage to pitch the ventures they have built and validated over the semester, in front of a panel of judges and a room of investors, mentors, and fellow students. Berkeley StEP is a hands-on incubator that helps early-stage founders go from idea to traction through customer discovery, MVP development, and real-world validation. StEP alumni teams have collectively raised $22.5M; alumni include Dispatch Goods, which closed a $7M Series A. What to expect: live pitches from the Fall 2026 cohort followed by judge Q&A, $6K in prizes awarded on stage, and time to meet the founders, judges, and mentors afterward. Judges: David Bloom (The House Fund), Bryce Gilleland (Cal Innovation Fund), Carly Anderson (Timescale VC), and Vikram Venkat (Cota Capital). Sponsored by Swift Ventures and O'Melveny. Who should come: investors and angels, startup operators, mentors and alumni, Berkeley students thinking about starting something, and anyone who likes watching founders pitch. Hosted by Berkeley StEP and the Berkeley Haas Entrepreneurship Program. Contact: Lynsey Inthasone, linthasone@berkeley.edu
